Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TasteMatch handles information when you use its restaurant discovery and recommendation website. This page is a project draft and must be reviewed against the application's actual data practices before public launch.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features enabled, TasteMatch may process information you provide when using the service, such as restaurant search terms, cuisine choices, price preferences, and other dining preferences. If account features are enabled, information needed to create and maintain your account may also be processed.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide restaurant search and recommendation features.
  • Apply the preferences selected by the user.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.
  • Diagnose errors and improve the website.

3. Restaurant information

Restaurant names, descriptions, ratings, images, and other listing details may originate from third-party sources or datasets. TasteMatch should identify relevant sources where required and does not guarantee that every listing is complete, current, or independently verified.

4. Cookies and technical data

The hosting environment and application configuration may process technical information such as server logs, browser requests, and error details. Whether cookies, analytics, or similar technologies are used must be confirmed from the deployed application and its hosting setup.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers required to operate the website. Before launch, the operator must document the providers in use, the information they receive, and any applicable data transfers. Do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking services without updating this policy where necessary.

6. Data retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the stated purpose or as required by applicable law. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the processing of your personal data. The process for making these requests must be provided by the service operator before launch.

8. Children's privacy

TasteMatch is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. The operator should establish appropriate age-related rules if the service is made available to children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The date at the top of this page should be updated whenever a revised version is published.

10. Contact

Before public launch, replace this section with the operator's real contact details and a working channel for privacy requests.
